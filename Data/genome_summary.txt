Summary
-------
The Arabidopsis thaliana chloroplast genome is a complete,
circular genome of 154,478 bp with a GC content of 36.29%.
The genome consists of one scaffold and one contig with no
sequence gaps and has a characteristic LSC-IR-SSC-IR structure.
Its annotation contains 129 annotated features representing
117 unique genes, including protein-coding genes, tRNA genes,
and rRNA genes. The annotation also identifies the pseudogenes
infA and rps16, duplicated genes associated with the inverted
repeat regions, and the trans-spliced rps12 gene.
