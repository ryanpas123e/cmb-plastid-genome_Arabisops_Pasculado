## Gene Content

The analysis identified 129 annotated features representing
117 unique genes, including 87 protein-coding genes,
37 tRNA genes, and 4 rRNA genes.

The annotation also identified the pseudogenes *infA* and
*rps16*, duplicated genes in the inverted repeat regions,
and the trans-spliced *rps12* gene.

## Important Observations

The chloroplast genome is circular and has a typical
quadripartite structure consisting of the large single-copy
region, two inverted repeats, and the small single-copy region.

The genome contains genes associated with photosynthesis,
ATP synthesis, electron transport, transcription, translation,
and other chloroplast functions.
